# Finding candidate references

For `brand-identity` step 4 when the member has no references, or only competitors. Run it fresh: galleries come and go, and what's distinctive changes. Bring back **6–9 candidates**, shown side by side so the member can point at what they like, each with its source link.

## Build the search from the brief

Before searching, write three lines from the work so far:

1. **Brand words** — the tone attributes and worldview from step 3 ("calm, precise, unsentimental"; "work should end on time").
2. **Adjacent categories** — worlds the customer also lives in, and worlds that share the brand's character but not its product. A bookkeeping service for designers → design studios, stationery, architecture firms. A meal-planning coach → cookbooks, grocers, kitchenware.
3. **The category default to avoid** — named in step 2.

## Three buckets

Fill each with 2–3 candidates so the set isn't one look:

| Bucket | What it is | Example search |
|---|---|---|
| **Adjacent** | Brands and products the customer already loves near this category | "[adjacent category] brand identity", "[adjacent category] website design" |
| **Far** | Outside digital products entirely — packaging, editorial, posters, signage, book covers, album art, architecture | "[brand word] packaging design", "[brand word] editorial layout", "[era or movement] poster" |
| **Character** | Things that literally embody one brand word | "calm interface design", "brutalist portfolio site", "warm minimal brand" |

## Where to look

Kinds of source, with places to start. Confirm a source is still live and still curated before relying on it; prefer the original page over a gallery's copy.

- **Curated web galleries** — sites such as Godly, Siteinspire, Land-book, Httpster, One Page Love. Good for live sites; screenshot the original, not the gallery card.
- **App and flow libraries** — such as Mobbin or Page Flows. Good for screen shapes; some need an account, so ask the member before signing up for anything.
- **Visual bookmarking** — Are.na, Savee, Cosmos, Pinterest. Good for far references and mood; noisy, so open each find at its origin.
- **Identity and type showcases** — Brand New (Under Consideration), Fonts In Use, The Brand Identity, design studios' own case studies. Good for type and logo direction; Fonts In Use names the typefaces.
- **Packaging and print** — Packaging of the World, publishers' and record labels' catalogues, museum and gallery identities.
- **Portfolio platforms** — Behance, Dribbble. Use for mood only: many shots are concepts never built for real content.

No web search or browsing available → say so, describe 6–9 candidates in words (brand, page, what to take from it), and ask the member to open the two or three that sound right.

## What to bring back

For each candidate: a screenshot (saved only once the member picks it), the source link, the bucket, and one line on what it could supply ("type: a condensed grotesque used huge"). Show them on the canvas or as an image grid in chat, numbered, with a recommendation: "I'd start with 2, 5 and 7 — they're the furthest from your competitors and still feel calm."

## Rules

- **No direct competitors** among the candidates. The member has seen them; they're the default.
- **No AI-generated moodboards as candidates.** They average the category; the point is a real thing someone made.
- **Cite everything.** Each picked reference keeps its source in `docs/3-BRAND.md` → Visual references.
- **Inspiration only.** References guide the direction; nothing from them (logos, photos, illustrations, copy) goes into the product.
