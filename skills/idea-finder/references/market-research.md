# Market research for a shortlist

How to research each candidate idea before scoring it, so the scores rest on evidence. Run it fresh every time — markets, prices and competitors move. Bring back 4–6 concrete data points across the shortlist, each labelled **[researched]** with its link, or **[member]** when it came from the member.

## What to look for, per candidate

| Look for | Where | Bring back |
|---|---|---|
| **2–3 comparable products** with a traction signal | Indie Hackers product and revenue pages, Starter Story interviews, Product Hunt, app-store listings and ratings, G2 / Capterra categories | Name, link, price, and the traction signal (revenue, ratings count, review count) |
| **What others with the same skill productized** | Searches like "[skill] software", "[profession] tool", "[craft] app", "[service] template"; vertical-SaaS lists; creator storefronts (Gumroad, Etsy digital, Lemon Squeezy) | Who did it, what form it took, what it sells for |
| **What people pay today** | Existing tools' pricing pages; what clients pay humans for the same job (rate cards, marketplace listings on Upwork / Fiverr / Contra, agency price pages) | A named price. The human price is the strongest anchor |
| **For craft or hobby ideas, what enthusiasts already buy** | Apps, subscriptions, coaching, courses, gear in that world | What they pay for, and roughly how much |
| **Public workarounds and complaints** | Subreddits, forums, Discord communities, shared spreadsheet templates, "how do you track X?" threads | Links showing people spend *time* on the problem |

## The absence check

A candidate with **no comparables and no current spend anywhere** fails the "no competitors = no market" rule ([validation-cheat-sheet.md](validation-cheat-sheet.md), principle 1). Cut it before scoring, and record the cut and the rule in `docs/1-IDEA.md` → Parked ideas.

## Trust and citing

- Prefer primary sources: a product's own pricing page, a founder's own revenue post, the listing itself. A search-result snippet is not a source — open the page.
- Founder-reported revenue is a signal, not a fact: say "reports" when quoting it.
- Put every page you used in `docs/1-IDEA.md` → Sources.
- No web search available: say so, ask the member for comparables they know, and label those points **[member, unverified]**.
