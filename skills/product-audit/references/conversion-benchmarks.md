# Conversion benchmarks

Numbers to cite in a finding's problem or recommendation, always with the source. **Last reviewed October 2026.** Figures go stale: re-verify with a web search before quoting one to the member as current. Where no primary source was ever traced, the line says so — quote those as "an industry estimate", never as fact. If web search isn't available, say so, quote with source and year, and mark it unverified. A benchmark sets severity; it never replaces what the screenshot shows.

## Speed

- LCP good <2.5 s, poor >4 s; INP good <200 ms, poor >500 ms; CLS good <0.1, poor >0.25 — Google Core Web Vitals.
- Every 100 ms of LCP delay ≈ 1.11% fewer conversions — Deloitte / eBay study.
- 53% of mobile visitors leave if a page takes over 3 s — widely repeated; no primary source traced.

## Front door

- Above-fold CTA + ~10-word value-prop headline: +96% engagement, 11.3% session-to-conversion — Hotjar 2026.
- Single-goal pages convert at 13.5% vs 10.5% with several CTAs — Landing Page Conversion Study 2026.
- First-person CTA ("Start my trial") vs second-person: +90% CTR — ContentVerve.
- Static heroes beat video heroes by ~7%; removing a stock-image hero beat keeping it by +4% — Landing Page Conversion Study 2026.
- 81% of best-in-class heroes show proof above the fold, vs 41% baseline — Landing Page Conversion Study 2026.
- Pricing hidden behind "Contact us" for deals under ~$25K/year: 90%+ conversion loss — practitioner estimate; no primary study traced.

## B2B SaaS funnel

- Visitor-to-lead: average 1.5–2.5%; good ~4%; top decile 8–15% — SaaS Hero 2026.
- Free-trial conversion median ~7.2% — SaaS Hero 2026.

## Forms

- Each unnecessary signup field costs roughly 10% of completions — industry estimate (form-abandonment research is often attributed to Baymard; verify).
- Email-only is the floor; more than three fields is the line of concern — practitioner consensus.
- A CAPTCHA on signup adds ~3% drop-off — industry estimate.

## Trust

- Five or more reviews make a purchase roughly 4× more likely — widely repeated; verify a current source.
- A 4.6+ store rating converts 30–50% better than 3.9 in the same category — industry estimate.

## Mobile and subscription apps

- 89.4% of trial starts happen on day 0 — RevenueCat 2026.
- Hard paywall median install-to-paid 12.11% vs freemium 2.18% — RevenueCat 2026 (verify).
- Paywalls with a trial convert at 64.5% vs 44.4% without — RevenueCat 2026 (verify).
- Leading the paywall with a 5-star review: +72% install-to-trial — RevenueCat 2026 (verify).
- Store listing view to install (iOS): floor ~15%, good ~25%, best 40%+ — industry estimate.
- A good preview video: +20–35% listing conversion — Gummicube (verify).

## Onboarding

- 3-step tours complete at ~72%, 7-step at ~16% — Chameleon benchmark.
- Day-1 retention baseline ~26%, optimised 40%+ — no source recorded; directional only.
