# AI distribution tools

How to pick the tool for a channel action or an automation, at the moment the member needs it. The tool market here turns over in months — names, prices, free tiers and whole companies change — so this file holds the categories and the way to choose, never a product list. Research the current options each time and cite them in the member's doc.

## Rules

- **A tool amplifies a channel that works; it doesn't replace one.** In Strategy, name a tool only where an action needs one (scheduling, a tagged link, a list). Most tool choices belong to Scale, for a proven winner.
- **One tool per bottleneck step.** Find the manual step that costs the member the most hours on the winning process, and automate that one.
- **Free tier or trial first.** No annual plan until the automation has held its guardrail metric for a cycle.
- **Every tool is optional.** For each tool a plan names, give the manual fallback in the same line.

## Start with what's already connected

Before searching the market, check what this harness can already do — it's free, already trusted, and runs where the member works:

- **Connected tools and plugins** (MCP servers, plugins, connectors): list what's installed. Common useful ones: a browser tool to research and monitor communities that have no API; a design tool for thumbnails, listing images and ad creative; a notes or docs tool for the content calendar; a CRM, sales-prospecting or email tool; a payments tool to read revenue; scheduled or recurring tasks to hold a cadence (draft today's post, pull this week's numbers).
- **Whether a connector exists** for the platform in question (the store console, the email tool, the ad platform, the analytics tool): search the harness's connector or plugin registry by the platform name.
- Anything the agent drafts for the outside world — a post, a reply, an email, an ad — is drafted for the member to review and send. Never post or send on their behalf without their say-so each time.

## The categories

| Category | Does | Worth it when | Keep human |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social scheduling | Queues posts across platforms on a cadence | Posting 3+ times a week on a channel that works | What's posted |
| Clipping and captions | Cuts long video into shorts, adds captions and effects | Short-form or long-form is a proven channel | The hook |
| Demo video | Turns a screen recording into a polished demo | Any product with screens, from Strategy onward | — |
| Social listening | Alerts when the pain or a competitor is mentioned in communities and social | Communities is a channel; the member can't read everything daily | The reply |
| Writing assistants | Drafts posts, threads and newsletters in the member's voice | Long-form or written social is a proven channel | The story and opinion |
| Search content and rank tracking | Keyword research, content scoring, position tracking | Search is a channel and pages exist to track | The angle and the final edit |
| AI-answer visibility | Tracks whether AI assistants name the product for a query | Search is a channel; worth a free one-off check early | — |
| Lead data and enrichment | Finds buyers by role, company and trigger; adds context | Outreach to a named list | The first line |
| Cold-email sending | Sequences, follow-ups, inbox warm-up and deliverability | Outreach volume past ~50 sends a week | Every reply |
| Creator discovery and management | Finds creators by audience, tracks codes and payments | Creators is a proven channel with 5+ creators | The brief and the relationship |
| Ad creative | Generates static and video ad variations | Ads, after an organic hook is proven | Choosing the winner to fund |
| Ad management | Runs and adjusts campaigns across platforms | Spend is meaningful and creative is proven | The budget ceiling |
| Email and lifecycle | Triggered sequences, newsletters, segments | From the first signups | The welcome email's voice |
| Referral and affiliate | Codes, tracking and payouts | After retention is proven | Who gets recruited |
| All-in-one GTM agents | One agent across several channels | Rarely for a first product — prefer one tool for the one bottleneck | — |

## How to pick a current tool

1. **Name the job in one line:** "clip each weekly podcast into five captioned shorts", "find 200 independent bakeries in the UK with an owner's email".
2. **Search for current options:** "best [category] tool [current year]", "[job] tool for solo founders", "[competitor of a known tool] alternative"; check review sites (G2, Capterra, Product Hunt) and recent founder posts. Skip listicles from the tools' own blogs.
3. **Shortlist 2–3 and check each on its own pricing and docs page:**
   - a free tier or trial that covers the job at the member's volume;
   - works with what the member already uses (their email tool, store, analytics, the harness's connectors);
   - recent activity (changelog or updates in the last few months) — this category is full of abandoned tools;
   - data and policy fit: no buying email lists; respects platform rules (automated posting and DMs get accounts banned on many communities and social platforms).
4. **Recommend one**, with the reason and the manual fallback, and let the member choose.

## What to bring back

For each tool in the member's doc: the name and link, what it does for this step, the price or free tier as checked on its own page with the date, and the manual fallback.
