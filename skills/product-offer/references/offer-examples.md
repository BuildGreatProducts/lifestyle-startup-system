# Offer examples

Five filled-in offers from real software businesses, one per business model. Use them to calibrate the *shape* of a strong answer — the named role, the number, the competitor — never as a script to retrofit the member onto. They are pre-AI-era; for AI-era anchors use [ai-era-patterns.md](ai-era-patterns.md). Customer counts, ratings and revenue are as publicly reported; re-verify any figure with a web search before quoting it to the member.

## Which example to reach for

| The member's product looks like… | Closest example | What to borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Their service, sold as a fixed package or subscription | Designjoy | A constraint as the mechanism; public revenue as proof |
| A tool for professionals who outgrew a big incumbent | Linear | A customer line with role, stage, size and the tools already tried |
| A focused utility for a daily workflow | CleanShot X | An outcome with a time number; proof from the store the buyer shops in |
| A tool sold to freelancers or agencies who build for clients | Bricks Builder | A pain you can reproduce and measure (a PageSpeed score) |
| A library, catalogue or two-sided product | Splice | A guarantee about what they keep after cancelling |

For a member productizing their own skill, Designjoy is usually the first anchor: one expert, one tightly scoped way of working, sold at a flat price, with proof built from the work itself.

## 1. Linear (B2B SaaS)

Project management for software teams that have outgrown Jira but need more structure than a Notion database.

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Engineering and product teams at fast-growing software startups (10–500 people) who feel slowed down by Jira's configuration sprawl but need more workflow rigor than Trello or Notion can provide. |
| Outcome | One always-current source of truth across PMs, designers and engineers — issues, projects and cycles updated in real time, so the team can plan, ship and review without status meetings or "is this ticket still right?" Slack pings. |
| Mechanism | A keyboard-first, opinionated workflow engine on a custom real-time sync layer. Sub-50ms loads, deep Git / Slack / Figma integrations, and curated defaults remove the configuration debt that bogs down Jira — the opinion is the product. |
| Guarantee | Free for unlimited members on the Standard plan, no credit card, no time-limited trial. Cancel paid plans in one click and keep read-only data. |
| Proof | Used by Vercel, Ramp, Mercury, Cash App, Loom, Scale AI and ~10,000 other software companies. 4.7/5 on G2 across 1,000+ reviews; case studies show team adoption in under a week. |

## 2. CleanShot X (indie Mac app)

Native macOS screen capture for power users who find Cmd+Shift+5 too limited and Loom too heavy.

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Mac-using designers, developers, support reps and creators who take screenshots and recordings dozens of times a day and want polished output without opening Photoshop or Loom each time. |
| Outcome | Capture, annotate and share a polished screenshot or recording in under 10 seconds — scrolling capture, GIF/MP4, instant cloud links, one-keystroke annotation. |
| Mechanism | A pure native Swift/AppKit app, Apple Silicon-optimised, built by a small team focused on one workflow: capture-to-share. Integrations with Setapp, Raycast, Alfred and Notion compound a head start over Electron alternatives that can't match the latency. |
| Guarantee | 14-day money-back, no questions. One-time purchase with lifetime updates on the desktop app; the cloud tier is opt-in, never required. |
| Proof | 4.9/5 across 3,000+ App Store ratings; Editor's Choice inside Setapp; featured in Wirecutter's screenshot-tools roundup and in creator workflow videos. |

## 3. Bricks Builder (WordPress plugin, lifetime licence)

A visual WordPress site builder sold as the lean alternative to Elementor Pro and Divi.

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | WordPress freelancers, web designers and small agencies building 5–50 client sites a year who want pixel control without per-site fees and without the bloat of Elementor or Divi. |
| Outcome | Build any custom WordPress site visually — headers, footers, dynamic templates, WooCommerce stores — that scores 95+ on PageSpeed Insights out of the box, with clean markup instead of nested shortcode soup. |
| Mechanism | A builder that outputs lean PHP/HTML directly instead of stacking shortcodes, with native query loops, dynamic data and a CSS-grid-first canvas. Built by developers who left other builders over their performance ceilings — the engine is the differentiator, not the UI chrome. |
| Guarantee | One-time payment, lifetime updates, unlimited sites — no annual renewal, no per-site activation. 30-day refund. |
| Proof | Voted #1 WordPress page builder in a 2024 community survey; thousands of five-star reviews in its Facebook community; public agency case studies cutting average page loads from ~3.5s to under 1s. |

## 4. Designjoy (productized design service)

Unlimited senior design work for one flat monthly fee, run by a single designer with no proposals or contracts.

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Bootstrapped SaaS founders, indie hackers and Series A startups who need consistent senior-level design — landing pages, app UI, brand assets — but can't justify a full-time hire or a six-month agency retainer. |
| Outcome | A polished design deliverable every business day, in Figma, across landing pages, app screens, brand systems and decks — unlimited revisions, under one flat monthly subscription. |
| Mechanism | One senior designer handles every account through a strict one-request-at-a-time queue. The constraint forces tight scope from the client, keeps quality consistent, and removes the proposals, scoping calls and statements of work that slow agencies. |
| Guarantee | Pause or cancel any time, no contract, no notice period — keep every file already delivered. Unlimited revisions until a deliverable lands. |
| Proof | Reported $1.5M+ annual revenue run solo, with monthly Stripe revenue screenshots posted publicly on X to 100K+ followers. Named client work (Castmagic, Tally, OpenPhone) and dozens of Product Hunt #1 launches; widely cited as the canonical productized-service case. |

## 5. Splice (two-sided marketplace)

A royalty-cleared sample marketplace with a credit-based subscription on top of direct producer sales.

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Bedroom and professional music producers in Ableton, FL Studio or Logic making electronic, hip-hop and pop, who need fresh, legally cleared samples without combing pirate forums or paying $40–60 per loop pack. |
| Outcome | One-click access to a 2M+ sample library — loops, one-shots, MIDI, presets — every sample cleared for commercial release on any streaming service the moment it's downloaded. |
| Mechanism | A credit-based subscription over a curated marketplace, with labels and named artists licensing packs per download. Similar-sound search and key/BPM filtering surface usable loops in seconds, replacing hours of browsing. |
| Guarantee | Cancel any time and keep every sample already downloaded — the licence survives the subscription. |
| Proof | 4M+ registered producers; samples credited on Grammy-winning records; native Ableton integration; recommended by producer YouTube channels such as Andrew Huang's. |
