# Start here

The Lifestyle Startup System turns your skill into a product you can scale, in nine steps. Each step is a conversation with your AI agent, and each writes one document to the `docs/` folder in your product's folder.

## Set up (once, a few minutes)

1. **Make or open your product's folder.** Starting from scratch? An empty folder is all you need.
2. **Install the system** as a plugin (see `README.md`), or copy this folder into your product folder as `lifestyle-startup-system/`.
3. **Open your agent in your product folder and say "start".** It sets up the folder and asks where you'd like to begin.

## The nine steps

1. **Idea finder** — find the product hiding in your skills.
2. **Product offer** — who it's for, what it promises, what form it takes, what it costs.
3. **Brand identity** — name, voice and a visual direction, explored on a canvas from references you like.
4. **Design system** — the colours, type and components everything is built with.
5. **Prototype flows** — the journeys that matter, then the key screens on a canvas.
6. **Build plan** — the plan your coding agent builds from.
7. **Security audit** — check the build before anyone else uses it.
8. **Go live** — one step at a time, ending with a test as a real customer.
9. **Go to market** — channels, then a list of actions, then experiments, then scale.

Already have a product? **Path finder** works out which of these steps you actually need, and in what order. **Product audit** reviews a working product screen by screen.

## The one word to remember

Say **"continue"** at the start of any session. Your agent tells you where you are, in plain words, and runs the next step. You can stop after any step.

## Where things live

- **`docs/`** in your product folder — everything the system writes for you, numbered by step. It's yours.
- **Your root `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md`** — a short guidelines block that keeps your coding agent faithful to your design, copy and build plan.
