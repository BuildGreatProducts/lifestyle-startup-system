# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-03

First release of the Lifestyle Startup System, rebuilt from ProductOS 2.0.0 for people turning a skill into a product.

- **Nine steps, one skill each**, numbered in order, each writing one document to `docs/`: idea finder, product offer, brand identity, design system, prototype flows, build plan, security audit, go live, go to market.
- **The skill is the step.** No phase folders, checklists, worksheets or routing files. Reference material moved into the skill that uses it, trimmed, and tagged `curated`, `checklist` or `research` with a `last-reviewed` date; anything that goes stale is researched live instead of stored.
- **One offer document.** Customer, offer, product and pricing are one skill and one document.
- **Design on a canvas.** Brand identity explores three directions side by side from the member's visual references; the design system builds from the chosen direction; prototype flows design the key screens with real copy.
- **A lean build plan** in place of the PRD and roadmap, modelled on a real plan: stack, key choices, architecture, data model, core logic, and phases that each end in a test you can run. Existing codebases get a current → target mode.
- **Security audit** gates go live; **go live** walks the member through one step at a time.
- **Go to market** keeps strategy, experiments and scale, written as actions to tick off.
- **Existing products:** `path-finder` scores what exists and writes the path; `product-audit` tears down every screen for UX, conversion, retention and product, with a canvas per flow.
- **`start`** replaces setup and continue.
- Removed from ProductOS: the four phase orchestrators, coach-composed plans, the Ship in 7 and Sell in 30 challenges, and the separate listing, prompt, eval, CRO, design-review and feature-finder skills (their useful material lives on as references).
